About Me

Hi, I'm Justin Ly — a Data Analyst and Computer Science & Engineering graduate from UC Merced. My work bridges data engineering and AI, with a focus on transforming raw information into business-ready insights.

I’ve built machine learning models for healthcare, developed ETL pipelines that saved hours of manual labor, and created dashboards that empowered teams to make smarter decisions. Whether it’s engineering database systems or deploying neural networks, I take pride in delivering measurable results.

I’m passionate about tackling real-world challenges through clean data, intelligent automation, and sharp visualization. Outside of coding, you’ll find me exploring new AI research or building tools that improve everyday workflows.
